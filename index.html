How Note Blocks works

  1. Pick a block — whole, half, quarter, eighth or sixteenth note (or a rest).
  2. Click a line or space on the staff. The note lands in the next free spot and you hear it.
  3. Fill each measure with exactly the beats the time signature asks for. The beat blocks under the staff show what’s left.
  4. Click a note to select it, then drag it up or down (or use ↑ ↓). Turn on Chord to stack notes.
  5. Play it back. With Loop on, keep building while it plays.

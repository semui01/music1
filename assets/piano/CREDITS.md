# Piano samples

These MP3 files are from the **Salamander Grand Piano V3** by Alexander Holm,
licensed under [Creative Commons Attribution 3.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The MP3 versions were taken from the [Tone.js audio repository](https://github.com/Tonejs/audio).

One sample is stored every minor third (A, C, D♯, F♯ from A1 to A6); notes in
between are made by pitch-shifting the nearest sample.
